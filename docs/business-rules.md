# ChairX — enforced business rules (P18–P20)

1. **Inventory**: cannot reserve/fulfill more than `available = onHand - reserved - blocked`. HOME and OFFICE counters are separate. Stock movements have operation IDs, preserve history and are not generated by GET.
2. **Receipt & FIFO**: a Purchase must be CONFIRMED; each receipt line belongs to that Purchase and cannot exceed remaining ordered quantity. Partial receipts are permitted in both warehouses. Cargo allocation is part of inventory cost; replays do not create duplicate lots.
3. **Purchase payments**: supplier amount ceiling = sum of `orderedQuantity × purchaseUnitCost`; cargo ceiling = specified `cargoCost`, with cargo pay blocked when undefined. Payments may be partial and occur before, during or after receipt. A payment must not mutate stock, cargo allocation or FIFO.
4. **Atomic financial payout**: a unique UUID idempotencyKey binds purchase ID, payment kind, account, normalized amount, reference and comment. Equal retries return the original result. Changed payload with the same key returns `PURCHASE_PAYMENT_IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` (409); money and document cannot be duplicated. A transaction holds the Purchase lock while checking the ceiling, inserting `purchase_payments`, and posting a single negative `PURCHASE_PAYMENT` journal entry. Failure rolls everything back. Different accounts use separate ledger balances.
5. **Purchase cancellation and cargo**: cannot cancel after any committed payout (`PAID_PURCHASE_CANNOT_CANCEL`) or physical receipt. Cargo may be updated while cost unlocked, but never below already-paid cargo. Refunds from suppliers are outside MVP.
6. **Payment channels (sale)**: `CASH` → CASH; `BANK_TRANSFER`, `MBANK`, and `BANK_INSTALLMENT` → BANK using existing PaymentMethod.TRANSFER. Bank installment is recorded `PAID` only *after* money reaches business bank account; no customer installment schedules or pre-settlement receivable simulation. Historical records with NULL channel fall back to CASH or BANK_TRANSFER on read, no backfill.
7. **Sale**: creation reserves from each source warehouse; fulfillment consumes that reservation with FIFO. Paid sale cannot be silently cancelled. Repeated sale creation uses its existing idempotency key.
8. **Delivery & returns**: Sale FULFILLED during dispatch does not imply that recipient accepted goods. For delivery sales, ordinary Return requires DELIVERED. READY, IN_TRANSIT or missing delivery returns 409 `DELIVERY_NOT_COMPLETED`; FAILED / physically returned failed delivery returns 409 `DELIVERY_RETURN_WORKFLOW_REQUIRED`. Failed shipment receipt is its own DeliveryService operation. FIFO's shared restoration budget prevents duplicate warehouse credits. Self pickup may return after fulfillment.
9. **Refund and exchange**: returned quantity cannot exceed sold quantity; linked refunds cannot exceed original value of returned items. Exchange settlement is the only allowed extra charge/refund for exchange-created sales.
10. **Money & close**: CASH and BANK change only via FinancePostingService/FinanceTransferService and signed `finance_movements`. Transfers net zero across accounts, are excluded from revenue and expense categories, and cannot spend nonexistent money. Opening balances do not count as income. The closed day prevents later postings for the business date.
11. **CashFlow**: uses `finance_movements.created_at` (posting date), half-open boundaries at Asia/Bishkek midnight. `totalIn=payments+exchangePayments`. `totalOut=refunds+exchangeRefunds+operatingExpenses+paymentCorrections+purchasePayments`. `netCashFlow=totalIn-totalOut`. The separately shown categories are *not* subtracted again.
12. **Defects**: open blocks available units and may preserve receipt-item origin; wait-for-parts changes no stock, resolve unblocks, write-off consumes valued stock after unblock. Closed defect cannot be closed twice. Operations are transactional.
13. **Authorization**: authenticated write operations use existing security middleware. Fine-grained purchase/defect/list permissions require explicit validation after integrating separately developed P17. No role/permission changes in P20.

All monetary examples are in Kyrgyz som. The project is an ERP for a small seller, not full banking accounting or a general-purpose inventory workflow engine.
